# IB PYP French Programme | Grades 2–5

**Author:** Junior BOOTO WABA · **Status:** portfolio design draft · **Language:** French as an additional language · **Context:** primary learners in Colombia · **Target:** A1, with support and extensions based on evidence.

This independently authored programme combines French language learning with inquiry, action and reflection. It is **not an IB-issued curriculum** or a claim that any school has approved these units. The suggested PYP connections must be mapped to the school's current programme of inquiry (POI) and language policy before classroom adoption. The internal stages below are planning markers, not official CEFR certifications or guaranteed outcomes.

## Design principles

- **Inquiry:** begin with an image, object, question or real situation; collect student questions and evidence.
- **Action:** each unit ends with a meaningful product or communication for a real or plausible audience.
- **Language:** teach listening, reading, spoken interaction/production and writing explicitly; visual literacy and mediation support these skills.
- **Agency:** children choose an image, role, example, partner or format where appropriate and reflect using observable « Je peux… » statements.
- **Access:** use images, gestures, audio replay, model sentences, word banks and optional extension; assess the same communicative goal with appropriate access supports.
- **Privacy:** publish only original teacher-created examples or synthetic samples. Do not upload student work, names, grades, school logos, internal documents or proprietary book pages without permission.

## Delivery model

Three terms, two units per term in each grade. Suggested length: 4–6 teaching sessions per unit, adaptable to the actual timetable. Each unit uses **opening → exploration and guided practice → action/product → four-skill evidence → reflection**. A short diagnostic in the first unit sets each learner's starting point; the grade label does not determine their CEFR level.

Start with [scope and sequence](curriculum/scope-sequence.md), then [24 proposed units](curriculum/units.md), [assessment framework](assessment/framework.md), and the [reusable planner](templates/unit-planner.md). Sources are documented in [references](references/sources.md).

## Adaptation checklist before use

1. Replace suggested PYP themes with the school's current POI, central ideas and lines of inquiry.
2. Confirm contact hours, calendar, available resources, accessibility needs and school assessment policy.
3. Review the initial diagnostic; select core objectives and extensions by evidence rather than presumed grade proficiency.
4. Prepare original audio, short reading texts, task sheets and exemplars; moderate rubrics with colleagues.
5. Record anonymous aggregate evidence and revise the sequence after each term.
