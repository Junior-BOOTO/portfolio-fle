# Scope and sequence: observable CEFR-aligned goals

These are **teacher-authored adaptations** for young learners, inspired by CEFR A1/Pre-A1 descriptors; A1.1/A1.2 are local planning labels and not universal certified CEFR levels. A1 is the overall target across the four grades. Grade 5 includes optional A2 bridge tasks only for learners who demonstrate readiness.

| Grade | Internal pathway | Listening (CO) | Reading (CE) | Spoken interaction / production (PO) | Writing (PE) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Pre-A1 → emerging A1 | I can identify familiar words and a simple instruction when spoken slowly with pictures/gestures. | I can match familiar printed words and short labels to pictures. | I can greet, name and answer a simple question using a word or model phrase. | I can copy/write familiar words and complete a model sentence. |
| 3 | emerging → consolidating A1 | I can find key facts in a brief, clear dialogue on routines and home life after replay. | I can locate familiar information in a short illustrated message. | I can ask/answer simple questions about daily tasks using rehearsed expressions. | I can write 2–4 connected simple sentences using a model. |
| 4 | consolidating A1 | I can identify people, places and actions in short, clear descriptions. | I can locate explicit facts in a simple illustrated description or notice. | I can describe a familiar place and ask for basic information with support. | I can write a short description or invitation with familiar connectors. |
| 5 | secure A1; optional A2 bridge | I can identify main points and explicit details in short, clear messages on familiar civic/environmental topics. | I can retrieve explicit information and distinguish simple reasons in a short text. | I can explain a simple choice or rule and take part in a short prepared exchange. | I can write a short message or poster with a reason and a proposed action. |

## Language progression

| Grade | Lexis and functions | Grammar and phonology focus | Transfer / inquiry |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | self, family, classroom, body, nature; name, identify, request | je/tu; c'est; être/avoir chunks; articles; singular/plural; sound–letter awareness | classify, observe, compare |
| 3 | routines, chores, food, community, seasons; describe, ask, sequence | present of frequent -er verbs; faire/aller chunks; negation; simple time markers | interview, record, sort evidence |
| 4 | landscapes, habitats, directions, stories, celebrations; locate, compare, describe | il y a; prepositions; adjective agreement in familiar phrases; et/mais/parce que | compare perspectives and places |
| 5 | rights/duties, nature protection, resources, francophone places; propose, justify | devoir/pouvoir chunks; il faut; imperative; simple cause/result with parce que/donc | investigate a local issue and propose action |

## Teaching and evidence cycle

Diagnostic → one focused formative check in each skill during learning → product/rehearsal feedback → four distinct summative opportunities → learner reflection and next goal. No single task or total score alone assigns a CEFR level. Use the same success criteria across versions with accessible scaffolds recorded.
